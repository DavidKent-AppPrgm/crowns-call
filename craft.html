Crafting & Building

A kingdom grows from the hard work of its people. Gather resources to build structures for decoration and defense. Craft tools, weapons, armor, magic, potions, and food to improve your luck in battle.

From the ground to the Inventory

Items live in an inventory you carry. A blueprint is an item with a job: it places a building into the world, and that building becomes a piece of the kingdom. The Fort Blueprint places the first piece of any kingdom.

The Inventory supports a wide range of armor choices to wear from Rings, Earrings, Necklaces, Helmets, L/R Spaulders, Bracers/Bracelets, Gloves, Cloaks, Chest Guards, Greaves, and Boots.

Each item can be improved by adding special items to the item slots of some items. Spices can be added to food in the same way that weapons and armor can be socketed with gems. Bags can be used to hold even more items.

Building

Buildings start as blueprints. Gather resources from the world, craft those resources into building blueprints, then use a blueprint to place the structure on your land. Each placed building becomes part of the kingdom.

People are essential to a kingdom. Residential buildings are how you bring them in: place homes, and people come to live under your reign. Special buildings can turn regular people into special people, giving the kingdom roles and strengths it did not have before.

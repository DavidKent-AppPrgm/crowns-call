Craft and Building

A kingdom grows from what the world gives up. Plants, trees, minerals, and animals are the stock of the land. You collect them, and blueprints turn that stock into structures you place yourself. The base those structures make is the kingdom. The names in the project are indexed under flora and fauna, minerals, creatures, and the base.

Getting Started

A new character is offered this quest the first time they enter, and the offer stays until it is accepted. It can be abandoned later from the quest log.

Getting Started. Welcome to the world. Collect 10 Wood.

Reward: 50 experience, and 1 Fort Blueprint.

From the pack to the ground

Items live in an inventory you carry. A blueprint is an item with a job: it places a building into the world, and that building is a piece of the base. The Fort Blueprint is the first of these a new character is handed.

Work also happens at stations already in the world. A grindstone is one of them. More recipes will be listed here as they are settled in the build. This page will not invent a recipe that the game does not have.

Library

The Base

The kingdom is the base you place. A blueprint that is also a building sits on the main terrain, at the same height above the ground every time, including on a slope. It may be set on grass, plants, rocks, and minerals. A tree in the way blocks it. A blueprint that is not a building can be placed more freely, including inside a structure, as long as it is not clipping through a solid wall.

The Base Control Pylon is part of that base. In the world it floats and turns.

Architectural sets

The project keeps several building libraries. The Human set is the one tied to the starting fort, and it also holds stalls and walls. The others are additional sets in the library. They are not separate player peoples.

  • Human
  • Elven
  • Dwarven
  • Goblin
  • Viking
  • Egyptian
  • Sandpeople
  • Vampire

Crafting trades

Gathered stock is worked through trades. Milling sits beside them and turns raw food into butters, flour, jams, meats, paste, powders, and tofu. The trades themselves, and the size of each library:

  • Smelting
  • Blacksmithing
  • Engineering
  • Tailoring
  • Retort
  • Alchemy
  • Cooking

Blacksmithing, cooking, engineering, tailoring, and alchemy are the largest of those libraries. Individual recipes are the next pass. This page does not invent a recipe the build does not already name.

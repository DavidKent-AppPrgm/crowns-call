Flora

Trees, fruits, and gathered plants on the generated world. Trees are listed here as kinds of tree. Every fruit and gathered plant links to its item page. The animals are indexed on Fauna.

Names here are the labels in the project, including spellings such as Shitake and Tumeric. Most plants have a few growth variants. Grass is its own thick set, used as ground cover after the plants are placed.

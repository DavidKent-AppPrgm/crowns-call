The record

I am building this because it is my life.

Crown's Call is my passion, and thanks to Unity and the amazing creators on the Unity asset store, I am making it as a solo developer. I hope to create a game that will bring friends and people together to enjoy as much as I do. You create your character and step onto your land. Play with friends, search for enemies, and fight them together. Gather resources, craft items, and construct buildings. Build your own world and defend it. This is the record of the game I love, written as I build it, so that anyone can play it.

Crown's Call is in development.

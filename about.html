About

One game. One name.

Crown's Call is being made by David Kent, alone, in Unity. The game is the brand. This website is where that brand speaks, and where the wiki keeps the record.

Two different sites

davidkentdeveloper.com is the portfolio of the person making the game. This site is for the game itself. News, rules, peoples, and systems belong here, under the Crown's Call name, even while one person is doing the work.

How the site is built

The pages are plain HTML and CSS, published with GitHub Pages from the crowns-call repository. A new wiki article is a new page that uses the same header, the same wiki list, and the same styles.

What comes next. A bestiary for the animals of the world, item pages, and a dungeons article can each sit beside World, Peoples, Craft, and Combat once those parts of the game exist. The shell is already here.

Crown's Call is in development.

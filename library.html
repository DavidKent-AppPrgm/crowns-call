How a world is laid down

A character’s world is generated in layers. Each layer waits until the one before it has finished. The order is terrain, rocks, minerals, trees, plants, grass, loose items, and then the player. Plants, grass, and items look for open ground. Trees stay off of rocks and minerals. Near coasts, the slope of the shore is what keeps those layers from floating.

A player can keep up to five characters. Each character keeps their own world, and their own quest record.

Combat

The fighting in the game today is player versus player. You fight in person, with a weapon in either hand, inside a stance. Magic sits in the same fight as steel. Armor is worn piece by piece.

Dungeons are later. Player-versus-environment dungeons are part of the plan for Crown's Call. They are not in the build yet, so this page describes the player-versus-player fighting that exists now.

Vitals

  • Health How much harm you can take.
  • Stamina The store you spend on effort.
  • Mana The store you spend on magic.

Attributes

Nine attributes sit under those vitals. Strength, Toughness, and Constitution. Agility, Dexterity, and Endurance. Intelligence, Wisdom, and Spirit. A people's starting spread is listed on the Peoples page.

Stances and hands

A stance is a way of fighting. It holds the abilities you can use, including the chains for the left hand and the right. Changing stance changes which motions are available. Items held in the hands follow that stance.

What you can wear

Armor and jewelry occupy their own places on the body: head, neck, ears, shoulders, gloves, bracers, rings, torso, back, leggings, and boots. The two hands carry what you fight with.

Conditions. A body can also be slowed by the world around it. Cold, wet, and frozen are tracked states, along with stealth and other effects. Individual spells will get their own entries when their names and costs are fixed.

The Game

A war you supply yourself.

Crown's Call is an online multiplayer game. You play on a team of five against another team of five. The map is rich with resources. You use them to craft, and you use what you craft to reach the other side's king.

How a match is won

The objective is direct. Eliminate the opposing team's king. Everything gathered on the map — wood, ore, plants, the buildings you raise — exists to make that possible and to keep the other team from doing it first.

What you bring into the field

Before the war, you make a character. You choose a people: Human, Elf, or Dwarf. You choose an allegiance: the Azure Accord or the Bloodbound. You shape a face, and you step into a body with health, stamina, and mana.

Those choices are the start of a character, not a locked class. Gear, stances, and what you craft change what that character can do once the match is underway.

In development. There is no public release yet. The wiki describes the game as it currently plays in the build, and those details will move as the game does.

The record

I am building this because it is my life.

Crown's Call is my passion, and I am making it alone. You create your character and step onto land that is already wild. You gather what grows there and what waits in the ground, and you raise a kingdom until a people look to you with favor. I want that world to feel worth staying in. These pages are the record of the game I love, written as I build it, so you can walk it with me.

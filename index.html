The Game Open the Wiki

The Match

Five and five

An online match is two teams. Each side fields five players. The fight is won by eliminating the other team's king.

The Wild

The wild map

The ground is full of resources. Wood, plants, and minerals are there to be gathered and carried back into the fight.

The Work

Craft, then press

What you gather becomes gear, structures, and advantages. A fort starts with a blueprint. A war starts with what you can hold.

The record

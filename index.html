The Game

Getting Started

Crown's Call starts with a character you create and a world that is generated for you. This world is full of plants, trees, minerals, and animals. Gather what it offers and spend resources making your kingdom stronger.

How a kingdom grows

Each new player must collect 10 Wood to build their Fort. The Fort is the first piece of your kingdom. Construct other buildings, stores, and defenses to attract people to your realm. Trade with them or train them for battle by building a Barracks. The choice is yours!

The Bifrost

Discover other worlds and claim their resources. Meet new people and learn new skills.

Defend your people

Defeat your enemies and protect your people. Grow the kingdom they live in, and hold it when others come for what you have built.

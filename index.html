Crown's Call

Create a character, step onto a generated world, and gather what lives there until the kingdom you raise can hold more people.

The Game Open the Wiki

You

A character you make

The game starts with someone you create. That character is the founder of the kingdom that follows.

The Land

A living world

You begin on a standard world. It is generated, relatively flat, and full of plants, trees, minerals, and animals.

The Kingdom

Build it larger

What you collect becomes a bigger base. A bigger base holds more people: characters of the world, and other players.

The record

This site is the public face of the game.

Crown's Call is the name of the game and the name of the brand built around it. The wiki writes down the world and the kingdom. The library is the first index of the plants, creatures, minerals, and building sets in the project. Player versus player is what you can play now. Dungeons come later.

Peoples

You begin by creating one character. That character founds the kingdom. The people who fill it afterward are both characters of the world and other players.

The character you create

Create the characters you want. Begin with a variety of avatar icons, appearance features, and character attributes. Everyone starts with 18 minor attribute points and 5 major attribute points.

Try the boards below: click a stat to spend a point and read what it does. Refresh to rebuild.

Who lives in the kingdom

Build residential buildings to populate the land with people. Build different buildings to utilize your people's skills. A larger base is meant to hold more people which will make your kingdom stronger. Invite other players to join the same kingdom with you.

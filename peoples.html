Peoples

You begin by creating one character. That character founds the kingdom. The people who fill it afterward are both characters of the world and other players.

Who lives in the kingdom

A larger base is meant to hold more people. Characters of the world are the people who belong to the land you are building on. Other players are people who join that kingdom with you. How those two groups arrive, and what work they do, will be written here as those systems settle.

The character you create

Humans begin quicker on their feet: agility, dexterity, and endurance start higher, and so does stamina.

Elves begin closer to magic: mana, intelligence, wisdom, and spirit start higher.

Dwarves begin harder to break: health, strength, toughness, and constitution start higher.

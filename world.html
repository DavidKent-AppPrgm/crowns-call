The World

The match is fought on a map you can live in. Land is generated, so the ground is not a single fixed board. Trees, plants, grass, and minerals are part of that land, and they are also things a player can take.

Settlements

Inhabited places are built at a scale. From smallest to largest, a settlement is a hamlet, a village, a town, or a city. Buildings in those places have jobs: houses, workshops, shops, farms, taverns, towers, and civic halls among them.

Players are not only visitors. A blueprint lets you place your own structures on the land you are fighting over. The first of those, given to a new character, is a fort.

What the wild is for

Resources on the map are how a team gets ahead. Gathering is the bridge between the landscape and the craft that feeds a fight. The opening request the world makes of a new character is simple: collect wood.

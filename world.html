The World

You start on a standard world. It is generated, and the ground is relatively flat. What fills it is lush: plants, trees, minerals, and animals. Those are the resources the land offers, and they are what a new kingdom is built from.

Your kingdom on that ground

The world is the ground. The kingdom is the structures you raise on it. A blueprint places a structure, the first of those is a Fort, given to a new character after they complete the quest 'Getting Started'.

As the base grows, it has room for more people. Some are characters of the world. Some are other players.
